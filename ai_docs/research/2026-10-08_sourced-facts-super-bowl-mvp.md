# Sourced facts for the Super Bowl MVP (2026-10-08)

Four Sonnet web agents, 10/8. Items marked **checked** I verified against the source text myself. Everything else is as reported by the agent with the URL given; "secondary" means news, blog or search summary, not a government or company primary page.

## City of LA Home-Sharing: fee, volume, denials
Source: NBS, *Home-Sharing Ordinance Fee Study*, Final Report, May 6, 2026, CF 14-1635-S7 Appendix A. https://cityclerk.lacity.org/onlinedocs/2014/14-1635-s10_misc_2_6-03-26.pdf (**checked**, 33 pp)
- **Current fee: $441** registration and renewal, adopted Feb 23, 2026, Ordinance No. 188796. Extended (administrative) $883; extended (discretionary) $12,798. (**checked**)
- Study's full-cost fees: $404 registration, **$454 renewal**, plus per-night fee. (**checked**) So the demo's $454 is the proposed renewal fee, and hosts' "$441–$454" is real.
- **$192 does not appear in the study** (**checked**). Our earlier "$192 verified" is wrong; drop it everywhere.
- Per-night fee $3.30 since Sept 1, 2025 (agent).
- New regular registrations (**checked**, study table):

| FY | Submitted | Approved | Denied | Denied % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 4,841 | 2,774 | 2,067 | 43% |
| 2023 | 5,624 | 3,048 | 2,576 | 46% |
| 2024 | 5,213 | 2,911 | 2,302 | 44% |
| 2025 | 4,619 | 2,574 | 2,045 | 44% |

  The 46% figure from the 9/18 session matches FY2023. Denial reasons are not in the study.
- Processing time: no official figure. Airbnb help page says "may take several weeks" (https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/864). Staff time is ~2 hours per application (study). No expedited process found.
- Requirements (Airbnb help page, secondary): photo ID plus one proof of primary residence if addresses match, else two; renters need a notarized landlord affidavit; RSO units ineligible; 120 nights/year unless Extended.
- TOT 14% (finance.lacity.gov, snippet only). Per-day fines not found (fine schedule PDF returned 403).
- Enforcement: 2024 LA Housing Dept report estimated 7,500 violating units vs 300 citations (via Shelterforce, secondary).

## Super Bowl and Olympics
- Super Bowl LXI at SoFi Stadium, **Inglewood**. CBS LA, 2023-12-13 (secondary). Date Feb 14, 2027 per Wikipedia only; not confirmed by NFL.
- LA28 Olympics July 14–30, 2028; Paralympics Aug 15–27, 2028 (la28.org).
- Super Bowl LVI (Feb 2022, SoFi): AirDNA counted 8,200+ LA-area rentals booked, ~$7.7M revenue, ~$316 ADR; 371 in Inglewood. Airbnb declined to give numbers (Spectrum News 2022-02-09, secondary).
- Later Super Bowls (AirDNA via trade press, secondary): Las Vegas 2024 nights sold +87%, ADR $437; New Orleans 2025 $10.5M, ~16,000 nights; Santa Clara 2026 ADR $415.
- World Cup 2026: Airbnb paid $750 to first-time hosts in some LA ZIP codes and ran a host workshop at SoFi (NBC, 2026-03-05). Inglewood active listings 103 (Aug 2025) → 180 (Jun 2026), per SAJE using Inside Airbnb (2026-07-14). No post-tournament results or permit-bottleneck reports found.

## Inglewood (where the stadium is)
- STRs legal since Ordinance 22-15 (July 19, 2022). Primary residence for the prior 365 consecutive days; unhosted "Vacation Rental" max 90 days/year (**checked**, ordinance PDF from the city).
- Fees $500 hosted / $600 unhosted, $500k liability insurance, processing up to 60 days with neighbor notice, TOT 15.5% (BNBCalc blog, July 2026, secondary; city pages not read). Airbnb help page says $50 (snippet). Unreconciled.
- Unincorporated County (includes Lennox): $914/year, primary residence, 90 unhosted nights, effective Oct 7, 2024 (ttc.lacounty.gov, primary).
- Hawthorne: STR license plus business license, 90-day vacation rental for non-residents (city FAQ, primary); fee not stated.

## Value per extra permitted listing (inferred)
- Airbnb take rate FY2025: revenue $12.2B / GBV $91.3B = 13.4% (secondary; 10-K not fetched).
- LA ADR ~$220, occupancy ~68% (AirDNA Aug 2026, search summary; direct fetch 403).
- A regular Home-Sharing registration caps at 120 nights: 120 × $220 × 13.4% ≈ **$3,500/year** per extra permitted listing.
- 2,045 new regular denials in FY2025. Cutting denials by a quarter ≈ 500 more permits/year ≈ $1.8M/year to Airbnb (inferred, illustrative).
