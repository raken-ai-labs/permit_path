# LA County STR jurisdiction survey (2026-10-16)

Question: regulatory status of short-term rentals (under 30 nights) in all 88 incorporated cities plus unincorporated LA County. Checked 2026-10-16.

## Read this first: confidence is low

- Six parallel agents surveyed 15 cities each; the table is stitched from their per-city notes. Many official hosts (city sites, municode, ecode360) returned HTTP 403 to automated fetch, so most rows rest on search snippets or on third-party code hosts (gocodebook.com, munirevs.com). Those rows carry `[UNVERIFIED]`.
- Rows marked `[NOT CHECKED]` were not reached. "unknown" means no STR provision was found, NOT that none exists.
- Roughly 10 rows are confirmed against a fetched city page or ordinance (for example Torrance, Claremont, Culver City, Diamond Bar, Gardena, Glendora, Covina, Lakewood, La Mirada, La Habra Heights, Malibu, Long Beach, Inglewood, Unincorporated County). Everything else needs a manual browser check before use.
- TOT is generally required wherever STR is lawful. Where a cell says unknown, the city's TOT chapter was not read.
- Conflict with the 2026-09-26 primer: West Hollywood is listed there as "owner-occupied, permitted"; this pass found only search snippets suggesting a ban. Treat as `[UNVERIFIED]` and re-check weho.org (403 to fetch). Also, Manhattan Beach's ban is contested (Keen v. City of Manhattan Beach, 2022, per search summary) in the Coastal Zone.
- Cities that use a third-party code host (gocodebook) are noted as such; confirm on the city site.
