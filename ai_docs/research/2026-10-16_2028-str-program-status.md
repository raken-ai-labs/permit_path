## What PLUM instructed (05/12/2026, Item 15, verbatim substance)
Instruct City Planning with the City Attorney to report back on a limited Vacation Rental Program consistent with CF 18-1246:
- Operate within the Home Sharing Program during major events, "including the 2027 Super Bowl and 2028 Olympic and Paralympic Games."
- Sunset no later than **December 31, 2028**.
- Citywide cap equal to **1% of current housing supply**.
- **250-foot separation** between units in Type 1 buildings (up to 4 units).
- **Pre-ownership:** host must have owned the property on or before **December 31, 2025**.
- Enforcement: photo ID and prior-year Schedule A or E tax forms; all documents under penalty of perjury; platform data sharing, SB 346, permit checks via County APN; **no permits for RSO or rent-restricted units**; add "individual trust" to the property restrictions.
- CAO fee study for full cost recovery.
Source: https://cityclerk.lacity.org/onlinedocs/2025/25-0029-S1_misc_05-12-26.pdf

## Eligibility terms as Planning describes them (draft baseline, not law)
From https://cityclerk.lacity.org/onlinedocs/2025/25-0029-S1_rpt_dcp_8-31-26.pdf (p.3):
- Must be a Dwelling Unit; not RSO or affordable-covenanted; not removed from the rental market under the Ellis Act in the past 7 years.
- Permits only to individual owners and property-owning trusts (no business entities); host resides there "occasionally or intermittently"; one Vacation Rental per host.
- Application: identity and ownership documents, Schedule A and/or E, affidavit under penalty of perjury that it is a second home.
- **60 booking days per calendar year** (the 2020 baseline; the 2026 PLUM instruction does not change this, but a new ordinance could).
- 1% cap citywide and per Community Plan Area; Type 1 max one per building plus the 250-ft rule; Type 2 (over 4 units) max 5% of units or 10, whichever is less.
- Planning recommends it be "parallel to, but independent of" the Home-Sharing Program, i.e., a separate permit type. It is not an amendment of the HSO primary-residence rule. Implication for PermitPath: if adopted, the primary-residence rule stays for HSO registrations and a new second product (Vacation Rental permit) appears. Confirm in the ordinance text.
