# Pasadena: short-term rental rules (research as of 2026-10-16)

Method: the adopted ordinance PDF and the city's August 2026 brochure were downloaded and read directly (pdftotext). The city's STR page was read through a summarizing fetch tool. The current Pasadena Municipal Code text (PMC 17.50.296 as amended, PMC 4.44) was not retrieved; a post-2018 amendment would not be visible to me. Anything not stated is marked `[UNVERIFIED]`. Not a legal opinion.

## 1. Status
- **Allowed: hosted and un-hosted home-sharing in the host's primary residence, with a permit.** Vacation rentals (a dwelling that is not a primary residence, rented short-term) are **prohibited** citywide. Ordinance 7317, 17.50.296(D); city page: "Vacation rentals are not permitted anywhere within the City." https://www.cityofpasadena.net/planning/short-term-rental-regulations/
- Permit Type 1 (hosted) and Type 2 (un-hosted). Un-hosted capped at 90 days per year.

## 2. Legal basis
- **Ordinance No. 7317**, adding **Pasadena Municipal Code (PMC) Section 17.50.296** (Zoning Code, Title 17). Adopted by Council **2018-01-08**, published 2018-01-11, effective 30 days after publication (about 2018-02-10, my computation). PDF: https://www.cityofpasadena.net/wp-content/uploads/sites/30/ORD-7317.pdf (saved: `ai_docs/refs/jurisdictions/pasadena/ord-7317.pdf`).
- TOT: **PMC Chapter 4.44** (cited in 17.50.296(G)(3)). Enforcement: PMC Chapters 1.24, 1.25 (esp. 1.25.160, 1.25.170); noise 9.36; litter 8.64.
- The city brochure (August 2026) calls it "Ordinance No. 7137", a typo for 7317. Brochure: `ai_docs/refs/jurisdictions/pasadena/STR-Regulations-Brochure-2026_final.pdf`.
- Amendments to 17.50.296 since 2018: none found on the city page; `[UNVERIFIED]`.

## 3. Permit or registration process and portal
- Portal (apply, renew, tax): https://portal.deckard.com/ca-los_angeles-city_of_pasadena-str-portal ; STR landing page https://www.cityofpasadena.net/planning/short-term-rental-regulations/
- Steps: complete the Supplemental Forms (notarized affidavit, property inspection self-certification, neighbor notice and list of notified addresses, photographs, proof of primary residency), apply online, pay, receive the permit number by email, show the number on all listings. (City page; brochure.)
- Eligibility conditions (17.50.296(E)(2)): Transient Occupancy Registration Certificate from Finance; notarized affidavit (primary residence; 24-hour local contact; fire and life safety; neighbor notice; tenant notified landlord); legal permit and habitability proof for accessory structures; indemnification agreement.
- Permit valid **one year** from issuance; not transferable; valid only at the original site; renewal requires fee, substantial compliance, updated information and the prior year's records (17.50.296(E)(3)).
- Inspection before issuance (self-certification or third party; accessory structures excepted) per brochure FAQ.
- Neighbor notice: delivered or mailed to all directly abutting owners (brochure).
- No separate business license required (city page).

## 4. Fees (application / renewal)
- Permit application **$123**, plus a **3% records management fee** and payment-processing fees (city page, read 2026-10-16). Fee set by Council resolution (17.50.296(H)); resolution number `[UNVERIFIED]`.
- Renewal fee: the city page does not state it; the brochure says holders "must pay an annual permit renewal fee." Amount `[UNVERIFIED]`.

## 5. Primary-residence requirement and proof
- Required. "Primary residence" = permanent residence or usual place of return, shown by **two** of: motor vehicle registration, driver's license, voter registration, tax documents showing the unit as the host's residence, utility or cellular phone bill, in the host's name; one primary residence per person; host must reside there at least **9 months per year** (17.50.296(B)(5)).
- Notarized affidavit that the unit is the primary residence (E)(2)(b)(i). Only the primary resident may hold the permit: the owner or, with the owner's approval, a tenant (brochure FAQ).

## 6. Eligible and ineligible unit types
- Eligible: single-family residences, duplexes, condominiums, townhomes, multi-family rental units, **except covenant-restricted (affordable) and Section 8 units** (17.50.296(C)). Single bedrooms or whole units.
- Owner-occupied multi-unit properties: owner may short-term rent the primary residence plus **one additional dwelling unit** (E)(2)(e).
- **ADUs and accessory structures permitted or built on or after 2017-01-01 are not eligible**; earlier legal accessory units on the same parcel can be used if the main structure is the host's permanent residence (E)(2)(c). Brochure: ADUs permitted on or after January 1, 2017, non-permitted structures, vacation homes, commercial units and non-habitable structures are not eligible.
- HOA/CC&R rules continue to apply (E)(2)(f).
- Rent-stabilized units: not addressed in 17.50.296 or the brochure. `[UNVERIFIED]`
- Parking must be on site; street parking for guests is not allowed (F)(7; brochure).

## 7. Night caps
- **Un-hosted stays: maximum 90 days per year; hosted stays: no limit** (17.50.296(F)(4)).
- Stays are 30 consecutive days or less.

## 8. Occupancy limits
- No more than **2 guests per bedroom plus 2 additional** at one time (F)(5). Commercial events and gatherings prohibited (F)(6).

## 9. TOT (rate and registration)
- **TOT 12.11%** on bookings from 2018-03-12; **TBID assessment 3.89%** for year one from 2023-07-01, then **4.89%** for 2024-07-01 through 2033-06-30. Registration (permit first, then the tax portal). Quarterly reports, even with no rentals; due Apr 20, Jul 20, Oct 20, Jan 20; penalties and interest from the 21st day after quarter-end; non-filers are not renewed. Online remittance only. (City page.) Records kept three years (17.50.296(G)(2)).
- **Platform collection:** from 2026-04-01 Airbnb and VRBO automatically collect and remit TOT and TBID; hosts must still file quarterly reports. The city page contradicts itself on whether platforms remit TBID; city says confirm with the City. `[UNVERIFIED: TBID collection by platforms]`

## 10. Insurance
- None required by the ordinance or stated on the city page. `[UNVERIFIED: none elsewhere]` Host signs an indemnification agreement for the City instead (17.50.296(E)(2)(g)).

## 11. Platform obligations
- Listings must carry the City permit number, including on platforms (F)(3). No person may advertise, book or facilitate non-compliant rentals (F)(2).
- Platforms collect and remit TOT (and per one notice TBID) from 2026-04-01 (city page). Data-sharing or "do not book unregistered listing" duties: not in the ordinance as read. `[UNVERIFIED]`
- "Hosting Platform" is defined in the ordinance, but no platform-specific penalties appear.

## 12. Penalties
- Violation of PMC (Chapter 1.24 general penalty), administrative fines and penalties under PMC 1.25.160 and 1.25.170; **three violations against the same property** may automatically suspend and, after a hearing, revoke the permit, with a one-year wait before reapplying; cost recovery, back TOT and disgorgement of revenue; daily fines per brochure (17.50.296(I)). Dollar amounts `[UNVERIFIED]`.

## 13. Processing time
- **1 to 3 weeks** for the emailed permit number after the online application (city page).

## 14. Date the rule last changed
- Ordinance 7317 adopted 2018-01-08. Latest operational changes: TBID 2023-07-01; platform tax collection 2026-04-01; brochure dated August 2026. Code amendments since 2018 `[UNVERIFIED]`.
