# West Hollywood: short-term rental rules (research as of 2026-10-16)

Method: city webpages and the Municipal Code (ecode360, the city's code host) were read directly in a browser session; text quoted or paraphrased below is from those pages. Anything the city does not state is marked `[UNVERIFIED]`. Not a legal opinion. The Ordinance 24-33 PDF was not saved (an early attempt returned an error page and was discarded); it is cited by URL only.

## 1. Status
- **Vacation rentals (un-hosted, 30 days or less): banned.** WHMC 19.36.331(A): "No person shall offer, facilitate an offer, aid, or provide a vacation rental to any transient." https://ecode360.com/43916115
- **Home sharing: allowed, hosted-only, owner-occupied**, under a Home Sharing Business License (Chapter 5.66). Exception in 19.36.331(D). https://ecode360.com/43904420
- Voter-protected: the council may amend 19.36.331 only to increase or expand the restrictions (19.36.331(E)); Ord. 24-33 re-enacted these provisions (code history notes).

## 3. Permit or registration process and portal
- Apply for a **Home Sharing Business License**; city page: https://www.weho.org/city-government/city-departments/community-safety/neighborhood-and-business-safety/business-licensing-and-permits/home-sharing-license
- Registration portal (owner-only page): https://secure.hostcompliance.com/west-hollywood-ca/permit-registration ; TOT payment through the Registration and Tax Payment Portal: https://safe.hostcompliance.com/west-hollywood-ca/permit-registration/welcome
- Application contents (5.66.030): name, address, 24-hour phone, email, SSN or tax ID; proof of primary residency at the address; names of everyone who uses the unit as primary residence; affirmation of the chapter; proof of a TOT Registration Certificate under Chapter 3.32; every advertisement URL. City page adds: proof of identity, ownership and residency.
- Operating rules (5.66.050): licensee resides in the unit throughout the stay; respond by phone within one hour and on site in a reasonable time; interior door signage with emergency numbers; smoke and CO detectors; exit routes for multi-story multifamily; fire extinguisher in kitchen; no use of City visitor parking passes by guests; no home sharing while under an open health and safety Notice of Violation or pest infestation; clean bedding.
- Ads must show the license number (5.66.040).

## 5. Primary-residence requirement and proof
- Required. "Primary residence" = a residential unit the owner or leaseholder resides in at least **270 days per year**; a host can have only one (5.66.010).
- "Reside" = present in the location at least **four hours within every 24-hour period** (5.66.010); the licensee must reside in the unit for the entire stay (5.66.050(1)).
- Proof: "proof of primary residency" and, per the city page, identity, ownership and residency documents; which document types are accepted is not stated. `[UNVERIFIED]`

## 6. Eligible and ineligible unit types
- City page: only a property **owner** who owns and resides in the unit is eligible; **renters are not eligible**.
- Code 5.66.020: no home sharing (1) without express written approval of the property owner and HOA; (2) **in any rental unit**; (3) **in any inclusionary or other income-restricted housing unit**; (4) in any space not approved for residential use (vehicle, trailer, tent, storage shed, garage); (5) at any property subject to the **Ellis Act within the prior seven years**; (6) without a valid business license.
- ADUs and duplexes: page text indicates non-owner-occupied units (such as ADUs or duplexes) must be leased for at least a year (a search-result summary of the city page; the page text I read does not contain this sentence). `[UNVERIFIED]` Code 19.36.275 "Residential Uses - One Year Lease Minimum" exists; not read.
- Rent-stabilized units: covered by the "rental unit" bar. Multifamily owner-occupied condos: not addressed beyond HOA approval and 5.66.100 (CIDs may prohibit).

## 7. Night caps
- No annual night cap stated in Chapter 5.66. `[UNVERIFIED: none elsewhere]`
- Stays: "thirty days or less" (home sharing definition).

## 8. Occupancy limits
- Maximum of **two people more than twice the number of bedrooms** on city or county records (5.66.050(8)); i.e., 2 x bedrooms + 2.

## 9. TOT (rate and registration)
- **12.5%**, per the city page; paid monthly through the portal; due last day of the month after the reporting period. Registration: a TOT Registration Certificate (Chapter 3.32) is a precondition of the home-sharing license (5.66.030(5)). Chapter 3.32 text and any rate-change history not read. `[UNVERIFIED: ordinance setting 12.5%]`

## 10. Insurance
- **$500,000** liability insurance, unless the host exclusively uses a platform providing equal or greater coverage (5.66.050(13)).

## 11. Platform obligations
- Chapter 5.66 imposes no platform duties beyond defining "short term rental platform" and requiring the license number in ads.
- For vacation rentals, 19.36.331(C): platforms may not complete a booking for a vacation rental in the city if they receive a fee, and may not take fees for ancillary services; safe harbor for compliant internet platforms; administrative subpoena duty remains. Subpoena power also in 5.66.090.
- Platform data-sharing and registry-check duties comparable to Los Angeles or Santa Monica: not stated. `[UNVERIFIED]`

## 12. Penalties
- Chapter 5.66 violations: administrative penalties under WHMC 1.08.030-1.08.070 (amounts not read `[UNVERIFIED]`); licensee is responsible for guests' citations; two or more violations or noise/nuisance violations in any 365-day period can mean immediate revocation and denial of business license renewal; fraud means immediate revocation plus a six-month bar; appeals under Chapter 5.08 (5.66.070).
- Vacation rentals (19.80.120): administrative fines under Chapter 1.08; **misdemeanor, fine up to $1,000 and/or up to six months** jail; investigative costs and back TOT; private injunctions with fee shifting; subpoenas.
