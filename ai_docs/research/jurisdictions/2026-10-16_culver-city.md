# Culver City: short-term rental rules (research as of 2026-10-16)

Method: the city's STR page and FAQ were read in full in a browser session (2026-10-16); the 2019 Subcommittee recommendations PDF was downloaded and read; the 2018 Airbnb voluntary collection agreement was downloaded and the first page read as an image (scanned). The Culver City Municipal Code (zoning table 17.210.015) was not opened. Anything not stated is marked `[UNVERIFIED]`. Not a legal opinion.

## 1. Status
- **Banned, with no registration program; reform is pending.** City page: "Short-term residential rentals (those of less than 30 days) are currently prohibited in City of Culver City ... no regulations have been approved at this time. It is also possible that the City Council may choose to ban short term rentals. It is anticipated that the City Council will consider and approve new regulations for short term rentals before the end of 2026." https://www.culvercity.gov/Have-Your-Say/Short-Term-Rentals
- **Soft enforcement:** the city says it "is not actively enforcing the ban ... by proactively identifying and citing short term rentals. That policy is subject to change at any time"; it still enforces nuisance and noise (same page FAQ). Listings on Airbnb or VRBO operate "without a permit and in violation of the Culver City Municipal Code."

## 2. Legal basis
- The Zoning Code is permissive; short stays are not listed as an allowed use in the residential land use table, **Culver City Municipal Code (CCMC) Section 17.210.015** ("Residential Zoning District Land Uses and Permit Requirements"), so they are prohibited (city FAQ). Section text not read. `[UNVERIFIED]`
- The Subcommittee memo says the ADU ordinance "currently prohibits renting ADUs for less than 30 days" (explicit prohibition since 2017). Source: `ai_docs/refs/jurisdictions/culver-city/str-subcommittee-policy-recommendations.pdf` (from https://www.culvercity.gov/files/assets/public/v/1/documents/city-manager/short-term-rentals/policy-recommendations-from-the-short-term-rentals-subcommittee_1.pdf).
- No STR ordinance number exists. TOT: CCMC Chapter 11.02 (Hotels and Motels), a document listed on the city page: https://culver-city.legistar.com/View.ashx?M=F&ID=4928026&GUID=48F9EF99-F4C0-49BC-9428-EDC53EC7D214 (not read).

## 6. Eligible and ineligible unit types
- None eligible now. Council direction of **2019-02-12** (not law; drafted ordinance never adopted) was to prepare an ordinance that would: allow STRs; limit them to the host's primary residence; allow hosted and un-hosted; no annual night cap; not allow STR of a duplex, triplex or apartment unit except the owner's on-site primary residence; neighbor notification; annual reporting. ADU policy was not decided. (City page; Subcommittee memo options A/B/C for ADUs.) Renters would have been allowed with landlord approval (Subcommittee).
