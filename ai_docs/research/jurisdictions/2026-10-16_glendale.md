# Glendale (California): short-term rental rules (research as of 2026-10-16)

Method: Glendale Municipal Code Chapter 5.56 and Chapter 4.32 were read directly on ecode360; the city's Home-Sharing page was read in a browser session; two ordinance PDFs from the city were downloaded and read (pdftotext). Note the search engine returns Glendale, Arizona for many queries; every source below is the California city (glendaleca.gov). Anything not confirmed is marked `[UNVERIFIED]`. Not a legal opinion.

## 1. Status
- **Hosted-only ("limited home-sharing") with a license; vacation rentals (no host on site) are prohibited in all zones.** GMC 5.56.010, 5.56.020(B). https://ecode360.com/43343384 ; city page https://www.glendaleca.gov/government/departments/community-development/neighborhood-services/licensing/home-sharing
- Home-sharing = an accessory use for stays of 30 consecutive days or less where **at least one host lives on site throughout the stay** (5.56.030).

## 2. Legal basis
- Glendale Municipal Code (GMC) **Chapter 5.56** "Home-Sharing License and Prohibition of Vacation Rentals" (current numbering; history note: **Ord. 5972 § 5, adopted 9-14-2021**); Chapter 4.32 (Transient Occupancy Tax); zoning cross-references in Title 30, esp. 30.34.080 (ADUs) and 30.60.030(O) (amortization). https://ecode360.com/43343384 , https://ecode360.com/43341842
- Originating ordinances: **Ord. 5941** (adds the home-sharing chapter and amends TOT, passed 2019-12-10, effective 30 days after passage; originally numbered Chapter 5.110) and **Ord. 5942** (Title 30 zoning, amortization). Both are in the city-published packet saved at `ai_docs/refs/jurisdictions/glendale/home-sharing-ordinances-5941-5942-council-packet.pdf` (https://www.glendaleca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/54719/638487725590100000 ; the packet includes council staff reports, fee-study text and public comment; its first ordinance is 5941; I did not locate Ord. 5972's own text).
- **Ord. 6034** (introduced 11/19/24, adopted **2024-12-03**) amended Title 30 for ADUs and re-stated the use tables: "Home-sharing, as defined in Section 5.56.030, is permitted subject to the restrictions in Chapter 5.56. Vacation rentals ... are prohibited in all zones." Several table rows in the ordinance still cite "5.110" (old numbering). PDF: https://ecode360.com/GL4929/laws/LF2224881.pdf (saved: `ordinance-adopted-2024-12-03-LF2224881.pdf`).

## 3. Permit or registration process and portal
- Portal: GlendalePermits.org (city page: "Apply for a home-sharing license at GlendalePermits.org"). Licensing Office (818) 937-8300.
- Application per GMC 5.04.050 plus 5.56.040(C): each host completes a Transient Occupancy Registration Certificate; proof of residency to the Community Development director's satisfaction; a designated local responsible contact or property manager available 24 hours; affidavit agreeing to the rules; written HOA approval where applicable; indemnification and hold-harmless agreement signed by owner and host.
- **Separate application for each dwelling unit** on a multi-unit lot (5.56.040(A)).
- License valid **one year** from issuance; non-transferable; expires automatically on any change of host or owner; a **new application** is filed on expiry (no renewal process) (original Ord. 5941 section 5.110.040(C); staff report in the saved packet). Whether current 5.56.040 retains that text `[UNVERIFIED]` (the ecode360 text I read omits it).
- Denial, suspension and revocation under 5.04.050 and 5.04.100; appeal under 5.04.110.

## 4. Fees (application / renewal)
- Home-sharing license fee set by Council resolution (5.56 text; Ord. 5941). The 2019 staff fee study put the cost at **$272** and proposed **no renewal fee** because renewal is a new application. Source: saved packet (staff report, "Fee Resolutions"). Current amount `[UNVERIFIED]` (fee resolution not located; the city page states no fee).

## 5. Primary-residence requirement and proof
- A "host" is a lawful occupier who has occupied the dwelling **at least 60 consecutive days** with intent to establish it as his or her residence; may be an owner or lessee (5.56.030).
- Proof of residency "to the satisfaction of the community development director" (5.56.040(C)(2)); document types are not listed in the code. `[UNVERIFIED: city checklist]`
- No days-per-year minimum comparable to LA's.

## 6. Eligible and ineligible unit types
- Eligible: single-family dwellings, condominiums, multiple residential dwellings, **except where prohibited by law or agreement**, including affordable-housing covenants, leases or rental agreements, and CC&Rs (5.56.020(A)). Renters may be hosts if the lease permits.
- **Not allowed on any property with an accessory dwelling unit, in either the primary dwelling or the ADU** (5.56.020(A), citing 30.34.080).
- Not allowed in non-residential space: vehicles, RVs, sheds, campers, trailers, garages, temporary structures (5.56.050(G)).
- Rent-stabilized units: Glendale has no rent-control program cited in these sources `[UNVERIFIED]`; deed-restricted units are excluded where the covenant prohibits.

## 9. TOT (rate and registration)
- **12%** of rent (GMC 4.32.030). Each host must complete a Transient Occupancy Registration Certificate as part of the application; 4.32 compliance required (5.56.050(J)); records for three years (5.56.050(I)); delinquency penalty 10% (4.32.080). https://ecode360.com/43341842

## 10. Insurance
- No insurance requirement in 5.56; the owner and host sign an indemnification agreement for the City (5.56.040(C)(6)). `[UNVERIFIED: none elsewhere]`

## 11. Platform obligations
- The code defines "hosting platform" but imposes no platform duties; the host must display the license number in every ad, including on platforms (5.56.050(D)); no booking or facilitating non-compliant rentals applies to "persons engaged in home-sharing" (5.56.050(C)). No registry-check or data-sharing duty found. `[UNVERIFIED]`
- Guests must be given a code of conduct (5.56.050(L)); no exterior signs; no commercial events.

## 12. Penalties
- Administrative citations and penalties (GMC Chapter 1.24 in Ord. 5941); cost reimbursement, back TOT and disgorgement of revenue on conviction; remedies cumulative. The 2019 staff report set fines in the highest tier: **$400 first, $1,000 second within one year, $2,000 third and subsequent** (saved packet). Current schedule `[UNVERIFIED]`. The director may deny, decline to renew, suspend or revoke on violations or nuisance (5.56.040(D)).
- Existing vacation rentals were given a roughly five-month amortization (to about May 31, 2020 per the staff report).
