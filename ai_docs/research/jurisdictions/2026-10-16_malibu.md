## 1. Status
- **Allowed with an owner's annual permit, including un-hosted whole-home rentals.** Un-hosted use remains lawful because the stricter **hosted-only** ordinance (Ord. 472) cannot take effect without Coastal Commission certification, which the Commission **denied on 2022-08-12**. City page: https://www.malibucity.org/str ; in-progress page: https://www.malibucity.org/1070/STR-Ordinances-In-Progress
- Multifamily buildings: STR is prohibited as illegal hotel, motel or bed-and-breakfast use (Ord. 468, Recital A; it "neither allows new uses nor prohibits use types as currently allowed"). The permit regime therefore applies to residential property where STR is otherwise permitted. Details of the multifamily rule in the current code `[UNVERIFIED]`.
- Pending: a Council Ad Hoc Committee was created on 2023-01-09 to bring back a recommendation addressing Coastal Commission feedback (city page). Outcome `[UNVERIFIED]`.

## 2. Legal basis

PDF: https://www.malibucity.org/DocumentCenter/View/27338/Malibu-Ordinance-No-468---Short-Term-Rental-Enforcement-Ordinance (saved: `ai_docs/refs/jurisdictions/malibu/ordinance-468-str-enforcement.pdf`).

PDFs saved: `ordinance-472-hosted-str.pdf`, `ccc-action-letter-2022-08-12-denial.pdf`.

## 3. Permit or registration process and portal
- Portal: http://secure.hostcompliance.com/malibu-ca/permit-registration (new and renewal). Mail-in alternative with a wet-signature application to City of Malibu STR Permit Program, 23825 Stuart Ranch Road. Support: STRSupport@malibucity.org, 310-456-2489 ext. 381.
- Requirements (17.55.010(C)): owner and agent contact; **valid OWTS (septic) operating permit or City compliance agreement** (required for permit validity from 2023-01-01; MMC 15.44, 17.55.020(C)); attestation of compliance; proof of TOT compliance under Chapter 3.24; every listing URL; attestation that CC&Rs or HOA do not prohibit STR and the association was notified; bedrooms and proposed occupancy; locations of rented units; grant deed (and operating agreement if an entity).
- One permit per legal lot or condominium unit (17.55.010(B)). Permit one year; renew at least 30 days before expiry; submitting renewal does not extend expiry. Amendments within 30 days of changes. Appeal within 30 days to the Planning Commission; no STR activity during appeal.
- Code of Conduct must be given to guests and posted inside the entrance (17.55.020(A)(7)).

No refunds on revocation (17.55.050(D)).

## 5. Primary-residence requirement and proof
- **None** under Ordinance 468: Council directed removal of the primary-resident requirement before adoption (Recital T). The permittee is the property **owner**. The hosted Ordinance 472 (not in force) would require a primary-residence owner on site (proof by two documents per its text).

## 6. Eligible and ineligible unit types
- Eligible: residential property with a valid permit specifying the rented dwelling units (17.55.010; 17.55.020(B)).
- Not allowed: vehicles, trailers, tents, sheds, garages, anything not approved as a dwelling unit or not listed on the permit (17.55.020(B)). Guest houses: Ord. 472 would allow hosted rental of guest houses; status under Ord. 468 `[UNVERIFIED]`.
- Multifamily: see Status; ADUs, rent-controlled and deed-restricted units: not addressed in 468 (Malibu has no rent control program in the sources read). `[UNVERIFIED]`
- Property is ineligible if it has outstanding code violations, unpaid TOT, or fails the OWTS requirement (17.55.030).

## 7. Night caps
- None in Ordinance 468.

## 8. Occupancy limits
- **Two people more than twice the number of bedrooms** on City or County records, **up to 14 people**, unless a special event permit (Chapter 5.34) is obtained (17.55.020(A)(8)). "Bedroom" definition: 70 sq ft habitable room with a door and bathroom access.

## 9. TOT (rate and registration)
- **15%** of rent or bill for occupancy of "any hotel, motel, hostel, or permitted short term rental" (MMC 3.24.030). https://ecode360.com/44331852 . Owner collects and remits in coordination with any platform (17.55.020(A)(2)); proof of Chapter 3.24 compliance required in the application. Remittance schedule `[UNVERIFIED]`.

## 10. Insurance
- Not stated in Ordinance 468. `[UNVERIFIED]`

## 11. Platform obligations
- MMC 17.55.070: platforms collect and remit TOT as the owner's agent; disclose listings, owner names, addresses, length of stay and price paid on a regular basis (subject to law); **must not complete a booking unless the unit is on the City's registry of valid permits**; must not take fees for ancillary services for unpermitted rentals; safe harbor and compliance-agreement path; interpreted subject to federal and state law.
- Ads must show the permit number and any ad without a valid number must be removed (17.55.010(A); 17.55.020(A)(9)).

## 12. Penalties
- Operating or advertising without a permit: **$1,000 per day or violation, or twice the advertised daily rate per day, whichever is higher**; other violations **$500 per day or the advertised daily rate, whichever is higher**; each day a separate offense; permit holder responsible for guest violations (17.55.080). Denial or revocation: 12-month bar, plus six more months per illegal rental after denial (17.55.050). Revocation grounds include two noise citations in 12 months or three listed citations in 12 months (17.55.030).

## 13. Processing time
- Not stated. City warns renewals filed late are "unlikely to be processed before the current permit expires." OWTS permits can take six months or longer.
