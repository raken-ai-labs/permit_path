# Burbank: short-term rental rules (research as of 2026-10-16)

Method: the city's Community Development FAQ page was read directly in a browser session; the city's 2021 STR FAQ PDF was downloaded and read (pdftotext); the Burbank Municipal Code on ecode360 was searched in-browser for short-term/vacation rental provisions (none found). The city's dedicated STR page (burbankca.gov/web/community-development/str) now returns "Page Not Found"; what it said earlier is known only from search-result snippets. Anything not confirmed is marked `[UNVERIFIED]`. Not a legal opinion.

## 1. Status
- **Banned (not a permitted use).** City FAQ: "Short term rentals (less than 30 days) are not permitted in Burbank"; report concerns to Building and Safety, Code Enforcement, (818) 238-5225. https://www.burbankca.gov/web/community-development/Frequently%20Asked%20Questions
- Basis: STR is not listed as a permitted use in the Zoning Code ("STRs are not allowed in Burbank because they are not listed as a permitted use in the City's Zoning Code"). City STR FAQ (2021): https://www.burbankca.gov/documents/173607/173622/20210101_STR_FAQ.pdf/352938d1-39c8-b9c9-93e7-1fffa10dde0c?t=1629907150540 (saved: `ai_docs/refs/jurisdictions/burbank/str-faq-2021-01-01.pdf`). The Burbank code on ecode360 contains no STR or vacation-rental section (in-browser search of https://ecode360.com/50860075 , 2026-10-16); the ban is by omission.
- Enforcement is complaint-driven (2021 FAQ, Q5). Whether that is still true `[UNVERIFIED]`.

## 2. Legal basis
- Burbank Municipal Code (BMC) Title 10 Zoning, e.g., BMC 10-1-602 (uses in R-1/R-1-H zones; STR not listed) per a 2020 city Q&A `[UNVERIFIED: I did not read 10-1-602 itself]`. https://ecode360.com/50860075
- No STR ordinance number exists: a proposed regulatory ordinance (2020-2021) was not adopted. City page history (as quoted in search results, page now offline): the Planning Board voted on 2020-12-14 not to move forward with the proposed permit ordinance; on 2021-08-23 it voted 4-0 to recommend an ordinance explicitly banning STRs with fines of $1,500 / $3,000 / $5,000. Whether Council adopted that explicit ban `[UNVERIFIED]` (a 2024 Burbank Leader headline reports the city "considers tightening vacation rental ban": https://outlooknewspapers.com/burbankleader/city-considers-tightening-vacation-rental-ban/article_71afa416-7b71-11ef-99b9-db3b845ce44b.html , not read; the code shows no such section).
- 2020 Council action: the 2021 FAQ says ADUs "were prohibited from being used as STRs by the City Council in 2020" (instrument not identified) `[UNVERIFIED: ordinance number]`.

## 3. Permit or registration process and portal
- None. No STR permit or portal exists. (The 2021 proposal would have required a business license; not adopted.)

## 4. Fees (application / renewal)
- None for STR (no program). Proposed-only figures (not law): annual license $286.45; application $201.85; cap of 600 licenses (2021 FAQ, "Proposed Regulations").

## 5. Primary-residence requirement and proof
- Not applicable; no program. Proposed-only (2021 FAQ): primary residence at least 8 months a year; renters need written landlord permission.

## 6. Eligible and ineligible unit types
- No unit type is eligible. Proposed (not adopted): single-family and multifamily in R-1 through R-4; ADUs prohibited.

## 7. Night caps
- Not applicable. Proposed (not adopted): 120 booking days per fiscal year.

## 8. Occupancy limits
- Not applicable. Proposed (not adopted): 2 adults per bedroom plus 2, max 16.

## 9. TOT (rate and registration)
- TOT is imposed on "hotel accommodation" (BMC 2-4-603, Chapter 2-4 Art. 6/19); the rate is set in the Burbank Fee Resolution and may not exceed 10 percent unless otherwise approved. Actual current rate `[UNVERIFIED]` (not read in the resolution). Whether hotel definitions would reach an illegal STR `[UNVERIFIED]`. https://ecode360.com/50602956
