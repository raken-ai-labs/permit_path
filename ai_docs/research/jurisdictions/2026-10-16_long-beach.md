# Long Beach: short-term rental rules (research as of 2026-10-16)

Method: the city's official STR page was read directly (HTML fetched, text extracted) on 2026-10-16; the Coastal Commission staff report (a state primary source) was read for ordinance numbers. The Municipal Code text on Municode would not render in either browser session, so the code chapter text was NOT read; everything the STR page does not state is marked `[UNVERIFIED]`. Not a legal opinion.

## 1. Status
- **Allowed, with registration, including un-hosted stays.** City page FAQ 1: "Yes. The current municipal code allows for unlimited Primary Residence registrations and a maximum of 800 Non-Primary Residence registrations citywide." https://www.longbeach.gov/lbcd/enforcement/strs/
- Two registration types: **Primary Residence** and **Non-Primary Residence**; one of each per individual. Hosted and un-hosted activity types.
- **Pending review:** the page carries a notice that the city "is currently reviewing its Short-Term Rental ordinance for possible updates" (contact cdhousingpolicy@longbeach.gov). Rules may change. Secondary sources claim a 2025 rewrite (ORD-25-0001) and a 2026 amendment (ORD-26-0004); the city page does not mention them. `[UNVERIFIED: treat as conflicting, re-check Municode]`
- Neighborhood opt-out: residents can petition (census block group basis) to ban un-hosted STRs in their area; landlords and HOAs can list buildings on a "Prohibited Buildings List" (city page; Coastal Commission report).

## 2. Legal basis
- Long Beach Municipal Code (LBMC) **Chapter 5.77** (Short-term rentals). Chapter number per the Coastal Commission staff report: https://documents.coastal.ca.gov/reports/2021/12/W13b/w13b-12-2021-report.pdf (saved: `ai_docs/refs/jurisdictions/long-beach/coastal-commission-2021-12-w13b-staff-report.pdf`). Sections referenced: 5.77.020.M (prohibited buildings), 5.77.030.B.6 (owner consent), 5.77.080 (un-hosted restriction petition) per that report and city forms.
- Ordinances: **ORD-20-0024** (original; adopted 2020-06-23 per the Coastal Commission report) and **ORD-20-0045** (revised, added un-hosted rules; read 2020-12-08, adopted 2020-12-15). Nuisance rules: LBMC Chapter 9.37 (cited by city page). Municode link given by the city: https://library.municode.com/ca/long_beach/ordinances/municipal_code?nodeId=1346216 (did not render for me).
- The coastal-zone portion needed Coastal Commission certification; the 2021 report says Chapter 5.77 then applied outside the coastal zone only. Current coastal status `[UNVERIFIED]`. The page links "California Coastal Commission Ordinance Modifications" and a Coastal Zone map.

## 3. Permit or registration process and portal
- Steps (city page): determine type; review eligibility; review operating requirements; gather documents; apply; post the registration number on every listing; collect TOT.
- Portal: `https://secure.hostcompliance.com/long-beach-ca/permit-registration/welcome` (the only HostCompliance link in the page HTML; page text refers to a "registration portal" and "TOT Portal").
- Primary Residence: apply directly. Non-Primary: first an "Application of Interest"; applicants get a Reservation or a Waiting List place while the 800 cap is reached.
- Registration valid one year from approval (FAQ 4). Renew about 30 days before expiry; approved renewals run to the original anniversary date.
- After review, a 45-day period applies to cure deficiencies or the application is invalid and the fee forfeited.
- Inspection of guest-accessible areas required before approval; City may also require inspection at renewal.
- Eligibility gates: no pending code enforcement actions; no unpaid code citation fees; owner consent (notarized) if applicant is not the owner.
- Forms (downloaded to `ai_docs/refs/jurisdictions/long-beach/`): Primary Residence Certification and Indemnification, STR Property Owner Authorization, Legal Entity Authority form, FAQs (2021-01-26).

## 4. Fees (application / renewal)
- **$545.00** to review a new application and **$545.00** to review a renewal; paid before review; non-refundable once reviewed; no separate registration fee (FAQ 3). City page, https://www.longbeach.gov/lbcd/enforcement/strs/ . Effective date of the amount `[UNVERIFIED]`.
- Occasional Event Permit: separate Special Events fee `[UNVERIFIED]`; max four per registration year.

## 5. Primary-residence requirement and proof
- Primary Residence registration: the unit must be on the same parcel as the applicant's primary residence; "Primary Residence" = permanent residence or usual place of return for at least **275 days** per calendar year (page also says "9 months").
- Proof: **two** of motor vehicle registration, driver's license, voter registration, tax documents showing the residence, utility bill, each matching the applicant's name and address; plus the Primary Residence Certification and Indemnification Form (the city does not accept digital signatures) and the LA County Assessor AIN.
- Non-Primary Residence: driver's license matching the application name; LLC/entity applicants need a notarized authority form and good standing with the California Secretary of State.
- Renters may register (primary residence) with a notarized Property Owner Authorization.

## 6. Eligible and ineligible unit types
- Qualified (or portion): single-family dwelling, individual units on a multi-family property, maid quarters, guest house, apartment.
- **Not qualified:** ADUs and JADUs, rumpus room, garage, storage room, pool house, casita, live-work unit. ADUs also do not count toward the unit-count limit.
- Units allowed per property by count of qualified dwelling units: 1-10 units: 1; 11-50: 10%; 51-100: 12%; 101+: 15%.
- Buildings on the Prohibited Buildings List cannot be registered (landlord or HOA self-certification, notarized; coastal-zone HOAs contact the city).
- Rent-controlled units: Long Beach has no rent-control program cited on the page. `[UNVERIFIED]` Deed-restricted/affordable units: not addressed. `[UNVERIFIED]`
- Tenants cannot be evicted to re-list a unit as an STR (page notice, citing existing law).

## 7. Night caps
- **Primary Residence, un-hosted: maximum 90 days per registration period.** Primary Residence hosted: unlimited. Non-Primary (hosted or un-hosted): unlimited days per registration period. Non-Primary count capped at **800** citywide. A report-level secondary claim of a 350 coastal-zone sub-cap is `[UNVERIFIED]` against the code (the Coastal Commission report discusses a proposal to allow up to 350 non-primary STRs in the coastal zone; adoption not confirmed).
- No minimum night stay (FAQ 6). STR = 1 to 30 consecutive nights.

## 8. Occupancy limits
- **Two persons per bedroom plus two more, max eight** per STR, counting hosts and children; studio counts as zero bedrooms (max two). Events above this need an Occasional Event Permit (max four per registration term). Pools, spas and hot tubs closed 10 p.m. to 7 a.m. No exterior advertising signs.

## 9. TOT (rate and registration)
- **13%** on applicable room rentals (city page), reported and paid monthly; due the 10th of the following month; late penalties 25% (from the 1st of the second month) and a further 50% (from the 1st of the third month). Must report even $0 months. Airbnb collects and remits under a voluntary collection agreement; other platforms: host collects and remits for receipts on or after 2021-12-01. Portal emails come from hostcompliance.com. Contact STR-TOT@LongBeach.gov. TOT code chapter `[UNVERIFIED]`.

## 10. Insurance
- Not stated on the city page. A 2020 news summary (secondary) reports $1 million liability proof or a platform with equivalent coverage. `[UNVERIFIED]`

## 11. Platform obligations
- City page: all ads, including hosting-platform listings, must show the city registration number.
- The Coastal Commission report notes the Council added "two hosting platform responsibilities" in 2020 but does not list them. A 2020 secondary summary says platforms must show registration numbers and file monthly reports. `[UNVERIFIED]`
- Airbnb: voluntary collection agreement for TOT (city page).

## 12. Penalties
- Not stated on the page. The Coastal Commission report mentions administrative citations; amounts `[UNVERIFIED]`. Repeated violations are treated as nuisance under LBMC 9.37. STR operators and property owners are jointly responsible for nuisance violations. A 24/7 complaint hotline exists: 562.568.8665.

## 13. Processing time
- Not stated numerically. "Timing of registration approval will be dependent on staff workload"; 45-day cure window after review. Petition mail-out takes two to three weeks (FAQ 9).

## 14. Date the rule last changed
- Last confirmed adoption: ORD-20-0045 on **2020-12-15** (Coastal Commission report). Page FAQs dated 2021-01-26. The city says a review is in progress; any 2025-2026 amendment `[UNVERIFIED]`.
