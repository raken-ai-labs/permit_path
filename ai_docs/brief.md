# PermitPath brief (lifted from the office_hours Airbnb README, 2026-10-15)

## What they want
Tool to help LA hosts get and keep STR permits. Survey says denials are mostly documentation (survey p6, self-reported); the City doesn't publish denial reasons. Plus capture experienced hosts' know-how for new hosts.

## Proposed MVP (10/8)
City of LA only. Address check, eligibility questions, real cost, typed document readiness check (no uploads), renewal reminders, monthly report vs a holdout. Live ~Nov 16 for the Super Bowl, follow-up ~Dec 14, then 6 months of run. Phase 2 priced separately: document upload, AI assistant, denial triage, dashboard, Inglewood and Greater LA. Details in ai_docs/research/2026-10-08_mvp-definition.md.

## Facts (sourced 10/8)
- Fee: $441 current (adopted Feb 23, 2026, Ord. 188796); $454 proposed renewal (fee study May 2026); $3.30/night; $89 is 2019. Earlier "$192" was wrong.
- New regular registrations denied 44% in FY2025 (2,045 of 4,619); 43–46% since 2022. The "46%" from 9/18 matches FY2023.
- No appeal of denial (confirmed earlier).
- Value per extra permitted listing ≈ $3,500/year to Airbnb (120 nights × $220 × 13.4%, inferred).
